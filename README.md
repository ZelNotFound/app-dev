# Your Sky – Series Review & Episode Guide

## 📺 Series Overview

**Your Sky** is a Thai university BL series about two students pretending to be in a relationship.

> **Series Info**: Thailand (2024)  
> **Length**: 12 hours  
> **Total Episodes**: 12 episodes  
> **Genre**: School romance  

### 🎬 About the Series

*Your Sky* is a cute & sweet BL drama about two university students who pretend to be a couple. The main character feels bothered after receiving an unwanted love confession from someone with powerful connections at the school. To solve this problem, he enters a fake relationship with his senior, hoping it can deter the advances. Although their romance is supposed to be phony, they develop genuine feelings over time.


### Main Characters

| Character | Role | Personality |
|-----------|------|-------------|
| **Teerak** | Main protagonist | Cheerful, easygoing, people pleaser |
| **Muenfah (Fah)** | Love interest | Lee's older brother, restaurant manager |
| **Oh** | Antagonist | Wealthy senior with family connections |
| **Lee** | Supporting | Teerak's friend, Fah's brother |

### Story Arc

#### 🚩 The Setup
- Teerak receives unwanted confession from **Oh**, whose mother runs the school
- Teerak struggles to reject Oh without causing trouble
- **Lee** rescues Teerak before he has to give a reply

#### ⛵ The Incident
- Oh hosts birthday party on a yacht
- Teerak, Joy, and Type attend despite warnings
- Oh spikes their drinks with alcohol
- **Fah** intervenes when Oh tries to take advantage of drunk Teerak

#### 💞 The Fake Relationship
- Fah claims to be Teerak's boyfriend to protect him
- Teerak and Fah decide to maintain the charade
- They keep the arrangement secret from friends
- Lee and Teerak's sister Babe discover the truth but play along

#### ❤️ Developing Feelings
- Couple selfies, dates, and gift exchanges
- Fake romance becomes complicated by real emotions
- The line between pretense and reality blurs

## 🍽️ Side Stories

### Restaurant Subplot
Fah manages an upscale restaurant and bar with:
- **Lee** (brother)
- **Real** and **Hia** (friends/co-managers)

The restaurant setting provides backdrop for:
- Real and Hia's evolving relationship
- Playful banter turning romantic
- Professional and personal boundaries blurring

## 🎭 Themes

- **Authenticity vs Pretense**
- **Social Pressure and Consent**
- **Friendship Turning to Romance**
- **Family and Responsibility**
